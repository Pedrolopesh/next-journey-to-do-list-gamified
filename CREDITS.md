## LPC art (Liberated Pixel Cup)

> **License up front:** only LPC art available under **CC0, OGA-BY 3.0 or CC-BY** is used. Art that is only CC-BY-SA or GPL is left out (it would force sharing derivatives). OGA-BY and CC-BY require **credit to the authors**.
>
> The character and cabin lab (`apps/banner/lab`) uses LPC art; the full list, with authors, the license used and links, is in [`apps/banner/lab/CREDITS-LPC.md`](apps/banner/lab/CREDITS-LPC.md) (generated by the import script).

The LPC assets already imported (character layers and scene tiles) are in
[`apps/banner/lab/CREDITS-LPC.md`](apps/banner/lab/CREDITS-LPC.md), with author, license used and link per file:

| Group                                         | Author                              | License used      | Link                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Character layers (body, hair, clothes, shoes) | several, listed in `CREDITS-LPC.md` | OGA-BY 3.0 or CC0 | [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/liberatedpixelcup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator) |
| Scene tiles (`tileset/original/Sharm`)        | Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm)             | CC-BY 3.0         | [LPC Base Assets](https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles)                            |

The other scene elements (throne, fence, helicopter, lampposts, cabin, braziers and similar) are
drawn in code by this project, with no third-party art.

Rules:

- Keep the exact attribution text the author asks for.
- CC-BY-SA and GPL have sharing obligations: check compatibility with store distribution
  (App Store and Google Play) before using the asset.
- Art derived from a CC-BY-SA asset stays CC-BY-SA.
